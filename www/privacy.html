W8M Privacy Policy

1. Scope and Roles

Effective date: September 8, 2026

This Policy applies to the W8M website, advertiser and publisher accounts, management workspaces, advertising and supply integrations, measurement links, support communications, and related security and operational processing. W8M Network Inc. ("W8M") is responsible for information it collects directly from account users.

When advertisers, publishers, or other partners process information about their end users through W8M, each party's role as controller, processor, business, or service provider depends on applicable law and the parties' written agreement. Partners remain responsible for their own notices, required permissions, and authority to send data to W8M.

2. Information We Collect

Account and Business Information

  • Contact name, business email, company or brand name, domain or App Bundle, address, and contact details;
  • One-way password hashes, login and session records, account status, permissions, verification, and security events;
  • Support requests, business approvals, contracts, accounting, settlement, and legally required records.

Platform Content and Configuration

  • Campaigns, ad groups, creatives, landing pages, targeting, budgets, bidder endpoints, and reporting settings;
  • Traffic sources, websites or Apps, ad slots, integration identifiers, floors, seller transparency, and review information;
  • Audience sets, creative material, URLs, feedback, and other business content you submit or upload.

Advertising, Measurement, and Device Information

  • Request time, ad slot, page or App context, coarse location, language, device and operating-system class, content category, and response result;
  • Applicable GDPR/TCF, GPP, US Privacy, COPPA, GPC, DNT, or limit-ad-tracking signals;
  • Advertising identifiers, user identifiers, or a random W8M browser identifier when appropriate personalization permission exists;
  • Impressions, clicks, auction outcomes, conversions or other configured events, plus deduplication, security, and traffic-quality evidence.

Network boundaries may receive an IP address and browser User-Agent to complete the connection, protect the service, and derive coarse location. The advertising application does not persist raw IP address and User-Agent as a user identity or combine them to create a substitute identifier.

3. Uses and Legal Bases

We process information only as needed to provide the service, perform agreements, comply with law, protect the legitimate interests of W8M and its partners, or obtain consent where applicable law requires it. Uses include:

  • Creating, verifying, securing, and supporting accounts and handling login, recovery, and permissions;
  • Matching advertising with inventory, applying budget and frequency rules, and returning ads or normal no-fill results;
  • Measuring impressions, clicks, and configured events, producing reports, and administering accounting and settlement;
  • Detecting automated registration, invalid traffic, abuse, security incidents, and service failures;
  • Conducting business approvals, audits, tax administration, disputes, and other legal obligations;
  • Maintaining, troubleshooting, and improving the platform, without using raw Google user data to train general-purpose artificial-intelligence models.

4. Advertising, Privacy Signals, and Cookies

W8M processes advertising requests contextually by default. Local matching may temporarily use compliant identifiers only when runtime configuration is complete and a request carries valid personalization permission recognized by the platform. Missing, denied, conflicting, or unsupported signals result in contextual processing; COPPA requests use restricted processing. Precise latitude and longitude are removed in every mode.

The platform recognizes supported GPC, DNT, device limit-ad-tracking, US Privacy, GPP, and GDPR/TCF signals. Contextual or restricted processing does not create or read W8M's advertising browser identifier and does not disclose user identifiers to external bidders. W8M independently sanitizes requests even when external bidding is enabled.

Account services use cookies necessary for sessions and security. The direct web-ad interface uses the random aofei_pz_uid only with recognized personalization permission. Cloudflare Turnstile may process browser and device telemetry under Cloudflare's policies to prevent automated registration and spam.

5. Disclosures and Service Providers

We may disclose only the information reasonably necessary for the purposes above to:

  • Service providers that perform hosting, networking, databases, email, security, analytics, or technical support for W8M;
  • Advertisers, publishers, and reviewed and enabled external DSP/ADX participants involved in an advertising transaction, limited to delivery, measurement, anti-fraud, and settlement needs;
  • Payment providers that host funding or payout onboarding if that feature is enabled; hosted providers, not W8M forms, collect full card numbers, bank-account details, and identity documents;
  • Authorities, advisers, or successors when necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate misuse, or complete a disclosed business transaction.

Current principal external services include Google Gmail API for account email and Cloudflare for human verification and network security. External services process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

W8M does not sell account contact information for monetary consideration. Some jurisdictions may define advertising-related disclosures as a "sale," "sharing," or targeted advertising. W8M applies contextual processing to supported opt-out signals and provides additional rights where applicable law requires.

6. Google User Data

W8M's operator authorizes the Gmail API solely to send registration, email-verification, password-recovery, and account-security messages from a platform-owned mailbox. W8M requests the gmail.send scope. It does not read Gmail messages, contacts, Google Drive files, or Google profile data, and it does not use Google API data for advertising targeting, sale, transfer, or training general-purpose artificial-intelligence models.

Google OAuth client credentials and refresh tokens remain in a restricted operational environment and are used only to obtain short-lived access tokens needed to send mail. W8M's use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Retention

We retain information for the shortest reasonable period needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, protect security, and meet legal duties. Current technical baselines generally include:

  • Raw IP address, User-Agent, precise location, and raw privacy strings in the bid application are processed in request memory only; network and security layers may retain necessary request metadata under controlled operational schedules;
  • Sanitized auction operational logs are normally retained for 168 hours, and pseudonymous public-registration quota keys for no more than 24 hours;
  • An authorized random browser identifier and uploaded audiences are retained for 30 days by default; pseudonymous frequency state expires after 90 idle days by default;
  • Signed tracking and replay-prevention state normally lasts about 24 hours; action-attribution and experiment data follows configured and reviewed periods;
  • Account, contract, accounting, settlement, security-audit, and dispute records follow the account lifecycle, written agreements, and applicable statutory schedules.

Backups and immutable audit records may delay deletion for a limited period. At the end of the applicable period, we delete, anonymize, or isolate data unless law or a legitimate preservation obligation requires otherwise.

8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. You may also have rights to learn the categories, sources, purposes, and recipients of information and to opt out of certain sale, sharing, or targeted-advertising processing. W8M will not discriminate against you for exercising a legally protected privacy right.

You may update some information through account tools or submit a request to support@w8m.com. Identify the relevant account role, website or App, and requested right. We may verify your identity and an agent's authority before disclosing or deleting information. If W8M processes information only for a partner, we may direct the request to that controller.

9. Security

W8M uses technical and organizational measures proportionate to risk, including encryption in transit, access control, password hashing, restricted keys, pseudonymization, request signing, log sanitization, least privilege, and security auditing. No method can guarantee absolute security. Contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorized access to an account or data.

10. Children's Privacy

W8M account services are intended for adults authorized to act for a business. They are not directed to child registration, and W8M does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through account forms. When an advertising request is marked for COPPA treatment, the platform uses restricted processing, does not use an identity cookie, and does not send the request to external bidders. Publishers must correctly identify child-directed services and meet applicable notice, consent, and other obligations.

11. International Processing

W8M, its partners, and service providers may process information outside your country or region. Where applicable law requires, we use contractual, organizational, or other lawful transfer mechanisms and require appropriate protection by recipients.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy as services, laws, or processing practices change. We will identify material changes through this page, account notice, or another appropriate channel and update the effective date. If a change requires new consent, we will request it before beginning the relevant processing.
